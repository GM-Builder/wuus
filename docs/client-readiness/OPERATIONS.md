# Operasional dan template klien pertama

## Rutinitas owner

Cek admin + inbox pukul 09.00 dan 17.00 WIB pada hari kerja. Kirim respons pertama dalam satu hari kerja; review yang diterima dalam dua hari kerja. Jika antrean melebihi dua review/hari, beri jadwal baru secara eksplisit. Belum ada notifikasi email otomatis; jangan menganggap inquiry akan datang sebagai email.

Catat privat: lead ID, negara, niche, sumber, tanggal balas, masalah, langkah berikutnya, tenggat, nilai proposal, deposit diterima, biaya langsung, jam kerja, pelunasan. Status `won` di admin berarti tahap hubungan; **bukan bukti pembayaran**. Uang diterima hanya dicatat setelah bank/payment account mengonfirmasi.

## Katalog scope awal — keputusan internal

| Paket | Harga uji, bukan tarif pasar terverifikasi | Batas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | €390 untuk 2 klien pertama; tarif setelah validasi €490 | 1 halaman, 1 bahasa Inggris, max 6 section, max 6 tipe kamar, max 20 foto, email/WhatsApp inquiry, 2 ronde revisi |
| Showcase | €590 untuk 2 klien pertama; tarif setelah validasi €690 | max 5 halaman, 1 bahasa Inggris, max 10 tipe kamar dan 35 foto, inquiry langsung, 2 ronde revisi |

Harga final dan mata uang selalu di proposal. Domain, hosting, translation, fotografi, booking engine, payment gateway, AI live, maintenance berulang, dan fee pembayaran dijelaskan terpisah. Tidak menjanjikan booking/pendapatan tertentu. Ekstensi bahasa adalah quote tambahan dengan terjemahan yang disediakan/disetujui klien.

Estimasi kalender: Starter 5–7 hari kerja, Showcase 7–10 hari kerja, **setelah** deposit dan aset lengkap. Batasi target kerja Starter ≤12 jam dan Showcase ≤20 jam sebagai asumsi awal; ukur aktual lalu revisi harga/scope. Hitung: pendapatan bersih = uang masuk − biaya payment/hosting/aset/pajak yang berlaku; margin per jam = pendapatan bersih ÷ jam aktual. Jangan mengabaikan biaya waktu owner.

## Template brief (English)

Business/property name; decision-maker and approval email; target guests and main language; current website; primary action guests should take; page/room list; opening hours/policies/contact details; approved logo/photos and proof of permission; two visual references; existing domain/hosting owner; deadline; budget/currency; required integrations; facts that WUUS must not invent.

## Template proposal / statement of work (English)

Project ID: [ID]. Client: [legal name/address]. Supplier: [verified owner/business identity].

Objective: [guest action/problem]. Deliverables: [numbered page/section list]. Exclusions: [list]. Client supplies: [approved content/assets/access]. Delivery estimate: [working days] after cleared deposit and complete assets. Approval contact: [name/email].

Price: [amount/currency]. Deposit: 50% before production work. Balance: 50% after preview approval, before production launch/source handover. External costs: [item, payer, renewal]. Payment instructions: [verified provider/account only]. Fees/tax and payable total: [explicit agreement].

Revisions: two consolidated rounds within the agreed page/feature scope. Each round is one written list from the approval contact. New pages, languages, features, or a changed design direction require a separate quote and timeline. Client feedback due within [3] working days; delays move the delivery date by agreement.

Acceptance: [links, content, inquiry delivery, responsive widths, ownership checklist]. Bug support: 14 calendar days after launch for defects against this scope; content changes, new functionality, third-party outages and ongoing maintenance quoted separately. Cancellation/refund terms: [written agreement based on work completed and applicable obligations; resolve before deposit]. Governing terms/disputes: [owner/client agree before signing]. No automatic acceptance by silence.

This template is an operational draft, not a jurisdiction-specific legal certification. Final terms and invoicing obligations require the owner's business/account details and applicable requirements.

## Invoice fields

Unique invoice ID/date; seller/client legal details; project and milestone; currency; subtotal; tax if applicable; fees agreed; total; due date; verified payment reference; payment status; receipt date. Use a separate receipt after cleared payment. Never send unverified SEPA/Wise account instructions.

## Change request

Requested change → existing scope reference → extra deliverable → extra fee/recurring fee → schedule impact → written client approval → invoice/deposit if applicable → implement. No implementation before commercial approval for out-of-scope changes.

## Delivery checklist

1. Assets and copy approved; demo/AI imagery is labelled and never presented as real client rooms without permission.
2. Content, contact destinations, inquiry validation and real delivery tested; mobile/desktop/keyboard reviewed; HTTPS/domain/canonical correct.
3. Credentials stay in owner's/client's accounts, never message plaintext passwords. Client owns domain and production hosting; grant least necessary temporary access.
4. Record dependencies/renewals/cost payer; export source and data backup; restore to a disposable environment; document deploy and rollback.
5. Client approves preview in writing; balance clears; launch; hand over repository/access, editing guide, 14-day defect support dates, and escalation email.
6. Remove temporary access after handover; obtain permission before portfolio/testimonial; schedule follow-up only with agreed scope.
